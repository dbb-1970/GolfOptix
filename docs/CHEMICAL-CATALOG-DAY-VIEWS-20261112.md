## Starter catalog

50 U.S. manufacturer-sourced references across seven categories. Counts: 12 fungicides, 12 herbicides, 4 insecticides, 8 fertilizers, 1 growth regulator, 10 wetting agents and 3 adjuvants. Five sources/brands: BASF, Envu, Syngenta, LebanonTurf and Aquatrols. Each record has a source URL, a checked date and a stable reference ID. This is a curated reference catalog, not a licensed live regulatory feed or proof of use authorization.

The Products & stock screen offers Browse chemical starter catalog and Add your own product. Search/filter by product, category and manufacturer, inspect the source, and prefill a new course form. An exact existing name/manufacturer opens the existing reference rather than replacing or duplicating it. Superintendents confirm the container formulation, registration/applicability and inventory unit, then save. Entries start unreviewed. No rates, application schedules, ingredient percentages, stock, site approval or safety review are assumed. Supplier product pages are not relabeled as archived label/SDS files. Inventory begins only after an opening-stock baseline is confirmed. Manual entry and CSV import remain available. Demo examples and course records remain separate from the public starter file.

## Calendar views

Day, Week, Month, Date range and Upcoming work are now direct choices on Maintenance Calendar. A month date, +more button or week heading opens an expanded daily list. Previous day, Next day, Back to month and a date picker keep navigation explicit. Custom ranges include both endpoints and use the existing maximum one-year span. Each expanded view can be printed with course/date context. Cards link to the source job or plan and display person, location, duration and a preview of instructions. Existing filters continue to apply.

Totals separate work orders, ordinary/chemical plans, service reminders and course events. Scheduled task durations are summed, not claimed as crew labor hours, availability or actual hours worked; events are excluded and unknown durations remain identified. Cancelled/skipped rows are excluded from active-work totals. No records are changed by selecting a view or date. Crew workers still use their authorized personal schedule and tickets; the manager Maintenance Calendar permission is unchanged.

Updated the English/Spanish demo tour and illustrated product/calendar guides. Mobile checks covered 390-pixel layout, not a physical-device touch test. No live Kings records, stock, chemicals, applications, employees or emails were changed.
