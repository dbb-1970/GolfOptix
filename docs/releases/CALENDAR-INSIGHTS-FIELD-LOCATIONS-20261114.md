# Calendar Insights and universal field locations — November 14, 2026

Calendar Insights uses course-authorized NWS daily and hourly forecasts, with the existing cache and source-host restrictions. Hourly feed failure leaves ordinary calendar forecasts available. Stale/old forecasts, incomplete daily coverage and missing required values suppress the relevant estimates. No paid service or account was added.

Reference evapotranspiration uses FAO-56 daily Penman–Monteith. Hourly forecast temperatures provide daily extrema; mean RH estimates actual vapor pressure; forecast wind is treated as measured at 10m and adjusted to 2m. Solar radiation uses the FAO missing-data temperature-range estimate (coefficient .16), and elevation defaults to zero. Both assumptions are disclosed. It is an estimated reference-grass demand, not observed water loss, turf-specific soil moisture, or an irrigation instruction. High drying demand compares a minimum three complete forecast days' median; a seasonal historical-normal service is not present. Source: https://www.fao.org/4/X0490E/x0490e08.htm and https://www.fao.org/4/X0490E/x0490e07.htm . NWS permits use of its open data: https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api . Field calibration against a local station remains advisable before operational reliance on estimated ET.

Insights flag high drying demand, forecast rain during watering/application/event windows, wind during applications, and heat during scheduled work. Wind/rain/heat thresholds are planning flags, not regulatory or product-specific limits. Candidate alternative work windows use available hourly data, then go through the existing occurrence/schedule editor with event/crew/equipment checks. Events themselves are not proposed for automatic time changes. Managers explicitly save changes; chemical label review and field assessment remain necessary. Moisture inspections use the existing course-process scheduling form. Dismissals require a reason and retain actor, forecast issue version and history. Refreshing to a new forecast makes its new insights reviewable. The public demo uses clearly labeled invented weather and never requests personal GPS.

Field Locations is available across existing workspaces, with contextual entry points for maps, equipment, work tickets and per-green pin sheets. It captures device positions on demand, entered WGS84 positions (reported receiver accuracy or explicit unknown accuracy), and manually placed map points. Permanent features, daily positions and temporary incidents are distinguished. Categories cover irrigation, landscaping, cups, equipment, incidents and Other. Coordinates, accuracy, capture time, editor, links, dates and previous values are retained. Resolving a disabled-equipment location does not change the equipment's permanent location or silently change its condition. Dated pin records do not claim measured cup yardages or automatically rewrite schematic pin setups.

Existing checked GPS map moves now support the existing map feature types rather than only sprinkler/valve symbols. Non-irrigation moves never attach or deform pipes. Coordinate capture alone does not relocate the map. Geographic markers display where checked alignment can project them; map-only points are explicitly non-geographic. Course-ground records can use any checked covering alignment. Existing atlas/source geometry remains intact.

Location writes use the Crew API's membership, origin, role, version, idempotency, conditional-save and readback checks. Workers can report daily/temporary locations and update their own records; permanent-feature edits, resolving/reopening records and manager-level course access remain protected. Worker projections include only their reports/assigned-work locations, not the roster or private costs. Saved field locations are in the same course document and existing full recovery snapshots. Device callbacks are invalidated when a capture is closed, superseded or hidden. No background tracking or external navigation-provider transmission occurs.

English/Spanish fixed controls use the existing local dictionary. Device-size browser review is not a field test of phone GPS or RTK receivers. Some dynamic guidance/source wording remains English. Exact GNSS accuracy, per-course alignment, application suitability and historical-normal ET are not newly certified by this release.
