# Homepage planned / emergency maintenance claim

Reviewed November 11, 2026.

The sixth homepage area distinguishes planned maintenance from unplanned emergency work.

Source: TMA Systems, *Study of Operational Maintenance Techniques*, printed page 4 (PDF page 5): https://www.fm-house.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Study-of-Operational-Maintenance-Techniques.pdf

The vendor paper states that a reactive maintenance strategy can cost up to eight times more than a proactive strategy over the long run. It does not supply underlying evidence for that specific multiplier. The homepage therefore attributes the statement, says up to, describes it as an industry estimate, and explicitly avoids presenting it as a measured golf-course benchmark or guaranteed saving.

Cross-check: National Academies (2024), *Quantifying the Impacts of Delayed Maintenance of Airport Assets: A Guide*, Chapter 2, page 8, describes a 3–10 times comparison as a common rule of thumb, rather than a universal measured ratio: https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/27960/chapter/3

A 2018 John Crane presentation instead refers to eight times longer for unplanned activities; do not confuse that time claim with a cost claim.
