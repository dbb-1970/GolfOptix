YOUR DAILY OPERATION, IN FOCUS

Know what needs doing.
Know who’s on it.

Bring the map, the maintenance plan and the people doing the work together. GolfOptix gives superintendents a clear path from finding a problem to planning, assigning and approving the job.

01 / IRRIGATION
◎

Know your water infrastructure.

Find sprinklers, lines, valves and pump houses on the course map. Keep labels, field corrections and repair history together, with open problems visible until closure is approved.

02 / LANDSCAPING
❧

Care for every part of the course.

Map trees, bunkers, water, gardens and rock features. Resize or reposition them as conditions change, report work on individual features, or assign area-wide jobs such as care for all bunkers.

03 / GARAGE
⚙

Keep the fleet ready for work.

Build your Garage from known equipment or add your own. Record hours, manage components, set availability, schedule service and reserve operating machines for crew tasks.

04 / CREW MANAGEMENT
↗

Clear assignments. Accountable work.

Build crew schedules and assign work orders with clear instructions. Workers record what they did and request closure; the superintendent or authorized assistant reviews and approves the finished work.

05 / MAINTENANCE CALENDAR
▤

A plan for the working day.

See watering, applications, landscape jobs, equipment tasks and pump service together. Coordinate planned maintenance and emergency work, with dates, locations and the people responsible.

06 / PLANNED & EMERGENCY WORK
↻

Plan ahead. Respond with confidence.

Separate planned maintenance from unplanned emergency work. Give each job an owner, keep its work history and require approval before closure—so urgent repairs stay visible alongside the work that helps prevent them.

The cost of reacting can add up: TMA Systems cites reactive maintenance costs of up to 8× those of a proactive strategy over time. An industry estimate, not a golf-course benchmark or a guaranteed saving. Read the maintenance paper ↗
