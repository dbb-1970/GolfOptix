YOUR DAILY OPERATION, IN FOCUS

Know what needs doing.
Know who’s on it.

Bring the map, the maintenance plan and the people doing the work together. GolfOptix gives superintendents a clear path from finding a problem to planning, assigning and approving the job.

01 / KNOW YOUR COURSE
◎

The whole course, in context.

Find irrigation, landscape features and pump houses from the overview or a single hole. Keep field corrections shared, with the identifiers and history your team needs.

02 / PLAN THE WORK
⌖

A calendar for course care.

Schedule watering, pesticide and fertilizer work alongside pump-station service. See planned maintenance and emergency work together, with clear dates, locations and instructions.

03 / ORGANIZE YOUR CREW
↗

Clear assignments. Accountable work.

Build crew schedules, assign tickets and keep workers focused on their own jobs. Record work performed, request closure and retain superintendent or assistant approval in the history.

04 / CARE FOR CRITICAL EQUIPMENT
▤

Pump stations, component by component.

Build each station’s equipment and component inventory. Record meter readings, plan service and track breakdowns while keeping station condition and past repairs easy to find.
