Approved course calendars save online for users with access to that track. Map repair notes and map edits currently stay on this browser and account. Public Test Drive changes are separate and temporary.

The equipment catalog will be a selection library. Browsing it won’t create inventory, usage or maintenance history. Your course’s serial numbers, readings and location details will stay private.
